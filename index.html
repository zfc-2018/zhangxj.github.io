About me

I am with the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Central South University, which I joined in July 2024. I received my Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2022, advised by Professor Min Tang, and my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Central South University in 2016. From 2022 to 2024, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Natural Sciences, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

I study numerical methods for partial differential equations, with particular interests in multiscale kinetic problems and asymptotic-preserving schemes. My work includes radiative transfer, radiation magnetohydrodynamics, particle methods, and proton transport.

Research Interests

  • Numerical methods for partial differential equations and multiscale problems
  • Asymptotic-preserving schemes for radiative transfer and radiation magnetohydrodynamics
  • Particle methods for kinetic equations in strongly magnetized regimes
  • Deterministic numerical methods for proton transport and dose calculation

Publications and Accepted Papers

  1. Asymptotic-Preserving Particle-In-Cell Method for the Magnetized Vlasov–Poisson–Fokker–Planck Equation

    Anjiao Gu, Xiaojiang Zhang

    Journal of Scientific Computing, 108, Article 49 (2026). [DOI]

    Asymptotic-preserving particle methods for a strongly magnetized kinetic equation, with analysis of their asymptotic properties and uniform convergence.

  2. Depth splitting method for the linear Boltzmann model of primary proton distribution

    Xiaojiang Zhang, Xuemin Bai, Min Tang

    Communications in Computational Physics, Accepted (2025). [arXiv]

    A deterministic solver combining scattering decomposition and depth splitting for proton transport, with dose distributions compared against Monte Carlo simulations.

    Preprint title: A deterministic solver for the linear Boltzmann model of a single mono-directional proton beam.

  3. A fully asymptotic preserving decomposed multi-group method for the frequency-dependent radiative transfer equations

    Xiaojiang Zhang, Peng Song, Yi Shi, Min Tang

    Journal of Computational Physics, 491, 112368 (2023). [DOI]

    A decomposed multi-group method with asymptotic-preserving frequency, time, and space discretizations in different radiation diffusion limits.

  4. A spatial-temporal asymptotic preserving scheme for radiation magnetohydrodynamics in the equilibrium and non-equilibrium diffusion limit

    Shi Jin, Min Tang, Xiaojiang Zhang

    Journal of Computational Physics, 452, 110895 (2022). [DOI]

    A decomposition of radiative intensity yields a space-time asymptotic-preserving scheme for both equilibrium and non-equilibrium diffusion limits.

  5. Accurate Front Capturing Asymptotic Preserving Scheme for Nonlinear Gray Radiative Transfer Equation

    Min Tang, Li Wang, Xiaojiang Zhang

    SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing, 43(3), B759–B783 (2021). [DOI]

    A prediction-correction-projection approach that captures front propagation in the nonlinear diffusion limit while preserving the free-streaming limit.

  6. Semi-implicit front capturing schemes for the degenerate nonlinear radiative diffusion equation

    Min Tang, Xiaojiang Zhang

    Journal of Computational Physics, 436, 110290 (2021). [DOI]

    Two semi-implicit front-capturing schemes for degenerate nonlinear radiative diffusion, examining accuracy, stability, and computational cost.
